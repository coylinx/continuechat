How it works

Switch assistants without starting over.

One answer stalls, the other might nail it. ContinueChat carries the conversation across so you don't have to copy, paste and re-explain.

  1. Click Continue

    Open any chat on chatgpt.com or claude.ai and press Continue in Claude (or ChatGPT) — the button sits right above the message box.

  2. Choose how much to bring

    The last 4, 8, 12 or 20 turns — or all of it — trimmed to fit, without ever cutting a code block or table in half.

  3. Review and send

    The other assistant opens in a new tab with the message box already filled in. Edit if you like, then press send yourself.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does ContinueChat send messages for me?

No. It fills in the message box on the other site and stops there. You read it, change it if you want, and press send yourself. If the box can't be filled, you get a "Copy context" button instead.

Where are my chats stored?

Only in your browser. There is no account and no server that stores your conversations, and there are no analytics. The one network request ContinueChat makes is to check a Pro license key. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

What counts as a "continue"?

Each time ContinueChat opens a chat on the other assistant with your context filled in. The Free plan includes 3 per month and resets on the 1st. A continue is only counted when the new tab actually opens. Pins, folders, search and Markdown/JSON export never use it.

What happens if my Pro plan ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your chats, folders and pins stay exactly as they are; only Pro actions (more continues, new folders past 5, PDF export) are locked until you renew.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel Pro Monthly anytime using the link in your Creem receipt email — you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Within 14 days of your first purchase, email support@coylin.com for a full refund. See the Terms for details.

Is ContinueChat made by OpenAI or Anthropic?

No. ContinueChat is an independent product made by CoyLin Studio. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by OpenAI or Anthropic.

Which browsers are supported?

Google Chrome 116 or newer on desktop. Other Chromium-based browsers such as Edge and Brave usually work, but we don't officially test them yet.
