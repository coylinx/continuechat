Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

In short

  • Your conversations, folders and search index are stored only in your browser.
  • No account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • The only request the extension sends is a Pro license check — never any chat content.

ContinueChat ("the extension") helps you organize, search, export, and continue your AI conversations on chatgpt.com and claude.ai. It is made by CoyLin Studio ("we", "us"). ContinueChat is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by OpenAI or Anthropic.

What we access

To work, the extension reads the conversation list and conversation content shown to you on chatgpt.com and claude.ai, only while you use those sites. To load your chats it uses the sign-in session you already have on those sites, the same way the sites themselves do; it never stores your login or sends it anywhere else.

Where your data lives

All conversation data, folders, pins, search indexes, and usage counters are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB / chrome.storage). We do not have servers that store your conversations. We never upload, sell, or share your conversation content.

The only network request we make

If you buy ContinueChat Pro, the extension sends your license key to our license relay (a Cloudflare Worker at continuechat-license-proxy.costacruz896.workers.dev) to activate it and re-check it about every 3 days. The relay forwards only the license key and an activation instance ID to our payment provider, Creem, and returns whether the key is valid. It never receives conversation content.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Creem, our merchant of record. Creem collects your name, email address, billing country and payment details to complete the order and send your receipt and license key; we do not see your card details. Creem shares your order details (such as your email address and what you bought) with us so we can support you. See Creem's privacy policy: https://www.creem.io/privacy.

This website

continuechat.coylin.com uses no cookies, analytics, or third-party scripts. Like most web servers, ours keeps standard access logs (IP address, browser user agent, requested page and time) for security and troubleshooting, and deletes them on a rolling basis.

What we do not do

No account or sign-up. No analytics, tracking, or advertising. No remote code.

Your control

You can export or delete all local data from Settings at any time. Uninstalling the extension removes its local data. Your conversations on ChatGPT and Claude are never modified or deleted by the extension.

Children

The extension is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date if this policy changes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@coylin.com
